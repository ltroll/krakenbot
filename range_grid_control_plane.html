Operator buy permissions

Set the zone where automatic buys are allowed
Floor · Don’t buy below. Candidates and resting buys below the enabled floor are rejected.
Ceiling · Don’t buy above. Candidates and resting buys above the enabled ceiling are rejected.
Inside the zone · Strategy decides. The active strategy still chooses levels, sizing, timing, and profit targets normally.
UseRuleBoundary price
Saving these rules does not create a ladder or force a trade. On the next bot cycle, open buys outside the allowed zone are canceled and new candidates outside it are skipped. Existing inventory and all sell orders remain untouched. Disable both rules to return to unrestricted strategy pricing.
