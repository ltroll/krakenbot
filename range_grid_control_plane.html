Operator buy targets

How operator targets work
1 · Choose levels. Check each row you want active and set its exact limit-buy price.
2 · Take control. Turn on Manual target mode and save. The targets replace automatic candidates and pending automatic buys are canceled.
3 · Wait for price. The bot posts exact-price GTC buys without chasing. A target above market waits until it can be placed without crossing.
4 · Complete the cycle. A fill gets that row’s net target plus fees. The level reopens after its sale; existing sells are untouched.
Lock spacing links only the buy prices: moving any one by $250 moves every level by the same $250. Names, enabled rows, and profit targets do not change.
UseNameBuy priceNet profit target
Manual prices are GTC limit buys and must be at or below market when submitted. A 0% net target is fee-adjusted break-even; the bot currently adds its configured round-trip fee allowance to the sell price. Profit targets apply only to new fills. Manual targets bypass forecast opinions but retain hard safety, cash, and inventory limits. HOLD cancels open buys on the next bot cycle, but existing sell orders remain untouched.
